# the-personal-mba
Edition: Portfolio/Penguin 10th Anniversary Edition paperback (white jacket, blue title, gold head band; same as site cover).
Reference: https://img.ricardostatic.ch/images/085eb48a-1199-4403-b4d6-db407930c4f9/t_1800x1350/the-personal-mba-10th-anniversary-edition-josh-kaufman (Ricardo.ch listing) - a hand-held photo of the spine lying flat, rotated to head-up and cropped. Local copy: refs-raw/mba-4.jpg.
Confidence: medium-high (clear straight photo of this edition, but small: spine only about 100 px wide, and cool white balance).
Real typeface: DIN-style bold sans (title and name), a slab italic (Rockwell-like) for "10th Anniversary Edition". Google substitutes: Barlow 700, Bitter 500 italic (new families: Barlow, Bitter).
Notes: THE and the gold-band text read across (horizontal), the rest reads along. Portfolio Penguin logo (an archer in a circle) is approximated with a gold ring plus tiny "PORTFOLIO PENGUIN" text. Colours are corrected from the photo's cool cast toward the cover (off-white, #3a96d8 blue, #ad8a50 gold).
