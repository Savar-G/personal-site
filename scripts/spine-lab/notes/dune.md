# dune
Edition: Ace Deluxe Hardcover (2019, ISBN 9780593099322), the site's planet-and-figure cover.
Reference: Amazon gallery image https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/81D6WG7PvrL._AC_UF1000,1000_QL80_.jpg (book lying flat, spine visible). Found by the lead, replacing the agent's inferred design.
Confidence: medium-high (clear but small, slightly curved).
Fonts: DUNE is a custom inline display face, drawn in Saira 600; FRANK HERBERT in Barlow 700, tracked.
Not drawn: the figure on the planet, DUNE's inline stripes and teal-to-green fade, the Ace triangle (text only).
