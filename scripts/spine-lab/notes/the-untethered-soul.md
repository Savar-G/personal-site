# the-untethered-soul
Edition: New Harbinger / Nortic Books trade paperback (pale aqua cover, horse on beach). Site cover is the "3 million copies sold" printing; the spine photo is an earlier "New York Times Bestseller" printing with the same cover art and spine design.
Reference: eBay listing photo of the open book with spine visible: https://www.ebay.com/itm/128054062198 (photo 2-3); local copy refs-raw/us-e2-3.jpg.
Confidence: medium (small, slightly curved spine in an open-book photo; layout and lettering clear; exact aqua is taken from the site cover because the photo is grey and dim).
Real typeface: Adobe Garamond-style roman, white, mixed case. Google substitute: Cormorant Garamond 700 (already in the lab).
Notes: the real paperback spine shows no publisher logo in the photo; none drawn. A hardcover gift edition exists with a ribbon and a different spine; not used.
