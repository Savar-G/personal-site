# sapiens
Edition: Harper US hardcover, 2015 (ISBN 9780062316097), cream jacket with the fingerprint over the "i"; same cover as the site.
Reference: eBay seller photo, listing https://www.ebay.com/itm/257298696049 (book standing, front facing the camera, spine seen at a steep angle on the left). refs/sapiens.jpg is cropped from it. A low-res photo of the Harper P.S. paperback box set (https://southamptonsagharborbooks.cdn.bibliopolis.com/pictures/184763_9.jpg) shows the same spine design straight on and confirms the cream panel has red margins on both sides.
Confidence: medium (angled, foreshortened photo of this edition; positions along the spine are measured, positions across are estimated).
Design: red ground (#c1211a), short cream rule near the head, SAPIENS in cream caps along the top half, a cream panel from 0.445 to the tail holding "Yuval Noah Harari" in dark brown italic.
Typeface: the jacket uses an old-style book serif (Arno/Sabon class). Substitute: Cormorant Garamond 600 and 600 italic (already on the site).
Not expressible / left out: the Harper fire-and-water logo (red disc) near the tail; too small and blurred in the photo to crop cleanly, so it is omitted rather than faked.
