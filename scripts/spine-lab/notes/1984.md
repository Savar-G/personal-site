# 1984
Edition: first US trade hardcover, Nineteen Eighty-Four (New York: Harcourt, Brace and Company, 1949; no ISBN; LCCN 49009278), first-state red jacket. 314 p., 21 cm (LC MARC record, Open Library OL6049335M).
Cover: flat scan of the red first-state jacket, https://d3525k1ryd2155.cloudfront.net/h/651/231/1500231651.0.x.jpg (Biblio), 1431x2000, cropped to the front panel (1259x1835).
Reference (refs/1984.jpg): straight-on spine of the same jacket design in its later blue state, https://pictures.abebooks.com/inventory/32442724925_2.jpg. Used for positions and sizes; head chipped (most of "George" lost).
Colour and cross-checks (red state): https://d3525k1ryd2155.cloudfront.net/h/128/102/1571102128.0.x.jpg (angled, clean), https://burnsiderarebooks.cdn.bibliopolis.com/pictures/140947907.jpg (angled), https://secondstorybooks.cdn.bibliopolis.com/pictures/1392774.jpg (frontal, faded spine). Red #b03832 sampled from the front panel; red copies' spines are mostly sun-faded.
Confidence: medium (clear straight photo of the same design, but the blue printing of the jacket; red colours from angled photos). Along-spine positions differ by up to 0.05 between photos; values are an average.
Fonts: the title is a hand-drawn condensed modern serif (Onyx-like), drawn in Imbue 700; "George Orwell" and "Harcourt, Brace and Company" are a hand-lettered brush script, drawn in Courgette 400 (slants more than the original).
Not drawn: the torn edge of the cream label and its ragged black shadow (drawn as straight blocks).
