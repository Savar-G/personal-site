# shoe-dog
Edition: Scribner US hardcover, 2016 (ISBN 9781501135910); same jacket design as the site cover (black, gold title).
Reference: https://maxlapin.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/img_2805.jpg (photo of the spine lying down; rotated 90 deg clockwise and cropped to refs/shoe-dog.jpg). The photo is cut off near the tail (about 80 percent of the spine visible), so the tail is NOT seen.
Confidence: medium-high for the text, colours, order and positions; low for the tail mark (SCRIBNER text is inferred from the publisher, not seen).
Typeface: Franklin Gothic / Trade Gothic style bold grotesque in gold foil. Substitute: Inter 800.
Layout: "PHIL KNIGHT" then a red Nike swoosh then "SHOE DOG", all along, head to tail, gold foil on near-black navy matte jacket. Positions were computed assuming the spine length is about 8.2 times its width (the ratio of the site's spine dims); measure suggests the visible part ends at about 0.78.
Not expressible: the swoosh (approximated by a thin red rect) and the foil sheen.
