# project-hail-mary
Edition: Ballantine Books US hardcover, 2021 (black jacket, gold planet art, same as site cover).
Reference: https://pictures.abebooks.com/inventory/31271893979_2.jpg (same photo as the-martian; right-hand spine, cropped and rotated 1.5 deg).
Confidence: high for layout (two stacked name lines at head, two title lines at the middle, Ballantine mark at tail); medium for exact colour (glossy, reflections; gold is foil).
Real typeface: custom wide geometric sans where A is a hollow triangle. Google substitute: Michroma 400 (new family).
Not expressed: the A glyphs are plain A, not the open triangle (a triangle glyph is not in a latin subset and the painter draws no outline shapes); gold foil sparkle; the Ballantine house logo is only a short grey bar above the BALLANTINE / BOOKS text.
