# cant-hurt-me
- Edition: Lioncrest Publishing hardcover/paperback (2018, "NY Times Best Seller" band), same design as site cover (site cover says 7 million; the photo is an earlier printing, same layout).
- Reference:  the studio packshot from yandex image search result #4 (hosted on a book-reseller site, 1800x2400 image, saved refs-raw/cant-hurt-me-y4.jpg). Crop widened to the site's spine aspect.
- Confidence: high for layout (clear photo of this design's spine). Medium for colour: the photo is warm tan (#b08865), the site cover band is gold (#b88c2d). I used #b38a50 between them.
- Real type: Gotham Bold-ish (condensed look in photo), tracked caps. Substitute: Oswald 700 (condensed sans; Montserrat was too wide along the spine).
- Not expressible: the Lioncrest lion logo (drawn as a small ring + LIONCREST text), and the tiny dagger icon between the two lines (drawn as a thin rule with a dot). Text sits left of centre on the real spine (x about 0.38) in the photo, which I copied.
