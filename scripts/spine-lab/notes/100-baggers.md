# 100-baggers
Edition: Laissez Faire Books hardcover with brown jacket and burlap-bag illustration (same cover art as the site's brown cover).
Reference: https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/71UTpcxE7ZL._SL1500_.jpg (Amazon seller photo, book standing at an angle; rotated 5 deg clockwise and cropped to the spine face, which is foreshortened).
Confidence: medium (clear photo of the right edition, but angled; photo colours are oversaturated so brown and tan were taken from the site cover).
Layout: MAYER in small spaced white caps at the head; "100-BAGGERS" in tan slab caps (hyphenated on the spine) at the middle; publisher mark (two interlocking rings) at the tail.
Typeface: Rockwell/Clarendon-style extra-bold slab -> Alfa Slab One 400 (added family); MAYER is a geometric sans (Gotham-like) -> Montserrat 600.
Not expressed: the faint burlap texture on the spine; the real mark is two linked rings (drawn as two plain rings).
