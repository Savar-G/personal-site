# red-rising
Edition: Del Rey US paperback (2014 trade, black cover with red wing); same spine design as the 2017 Del Rey 3-book box set paperbacks.
Reference: https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/81KV3no3%2BfL._SL1500_.jpg (Amazon "Red Rising series" Del Rey box set; spine cropped from an angled box photo, so slight perspective skew).
Confidence: medium (clear but angled/skewed photo of the matching design).

Not expressible: the glow on the title, the Mars symbol (approximated by a ring and small rects), the Del Rey house logo (circle plus two small white rects), slight slant of the red/black boundary.
