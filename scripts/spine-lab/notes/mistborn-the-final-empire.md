# mistborn-the-final-empire
Edition: Tor mass-market paperback (2006-2008 printings, "Mistborn" / "The Final Empire" cover as on the site).
Reference: https://i.redd.it/18g7gsbh4x581.jpg (Reddit r/brandonsanderson, "Mistborn: the 1st mass-market paperback cover!"; angled desk photo of the exact edition, rotated -45 deg and cropped to refs/mistborn-the-final-empire.jpg; heavily foreshortened across the width). A second eBay photo (Tor fantasy shield at head, ornate "Mistborn" along the spine, author at tail) confirms the layout.
Confidence: medium-high on layout and colours (clear photo of this edition), medium on exact proportions (foreshortening).
Real spine: dark brown-maroon (warm rust at head, near-black at tail), Tor Fantasy shield logo at the head, ornate icy-blue "Mistborn" along head to tail, a white ornate scroll-work frame holding spine artwork, then ornate blue "Brandon Sanderson" on two lines along the spine.
Typeface: decorative calligraphic display face; Google substitute Almendra 400 (new family, added via getfont).
