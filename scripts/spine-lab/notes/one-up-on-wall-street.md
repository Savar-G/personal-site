# one-up-on-wall-street
Edition: Simon & Schuster first US trade hardcover, 1989 (ISBN 0-671-66103-5 / 978-0671661038), silver-grey jacket with red title, Lynch portrait, and the printed "The #1 Money Manager - Time" corner.
Cover: archive.org scan https://archive.org/download/oneuponwallstree00lync_0/page/cover_w2400.jpg (3380x5121, flat, no stickers).
Reference: https://pictures.abebooks.com/inventory/30506148881.jpg (Raptis Rare Books photo of a signed first edition, spine straight and visible; cropped, no rotation). Same layout on https://www.raptisrarebooks.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/RRB-150037.jpg (same listing).
Confidence: high (clear straight photo of this edition's spine; it is small, about 66x722 px, so sizes are measured to about 2 px).
Layout: title along the spine in red in the cover's style: large initial caps O, U, W, S with smaller caps for the rest; "ON" small, between two red rules. "Peter Lynch" in cream along the spine below it. Simon & Schuster sower mark with "SIMON AND SCHUSTER" at the tail, cropped from the photo (imgs/one-up-on-wall-street-ss.png, black ink on transparent).
Typeface: a condensed bracketed serif close to ITC Cheltenham / Century Condensed -> Source Serif 4 600 (title) and 700 (author). Word widths match the photo within about 5% at the measured cap heights; "Peter Lynch" is set with max to keep its real length, so its caps are a little smaller than the real ones.
Colour: the photo's title looks pink from glare and fading; the red is taken from the cover scan (#c41f24). Grey from the photo, darkened toward the cover scan's silver.
Not expressed: the metallic silver sheen of the jacket; the mark is low resolution (blurry at close zoom).
